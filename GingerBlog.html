Fuzhou - Shenyang | Going to Shenyang just for a music concert and "suffering" for 10 hours

Going to the northeast park of China has always been one of my dream since I started to plan out my solo China visit alone. When I heard that Qingfeng Wu is a lot of his other friends are going to be in the Shenyang Music concert, I knew there is another reason for me to go now. Not going lie it was kinda nervous for some reason but also really happy for it, imagining myself listening to their music in-person is crazy. First day in Shenyang was like 8pm at night and I went straight to the hotel and got some food on the way. One thing is that the cost of things comparing to Fuzhou is CRAZY! It was CHEAP.
